What is a Cutoff?

A cutoff is the last admitted candidate’s percentile for a college, course, category, and CAP round. It helps show the previous level of competition.

💡

How to Use This Page

1 Choose the admission year.
2 Select a college and course.
3 Optionally narrow by category code and CAP round.
4 Check the recorded cutoff values.
📖

Understanding Category Codes

Category codes combine seat type, candidate group, and location. For example, NGOPENH means Non-Technical, General, OPEN category, and Home District seat. A shorter code such as GOPEN means General Open category.
