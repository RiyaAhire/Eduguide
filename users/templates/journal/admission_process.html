THE COMPLETE JOURNEY

How Does Admission Take Place?

Follow the process from your first application to confirmation of admission at the institute.

01

Online Registration

Register through the official admission portal and create your application.

  • Enter your basic information.
  • Provide academic details.
  • Enter category/candidature information where applicable.
  • Use an active mobile number and email.
Tip: Check every detail carefully before proceeding.
02

Application & Document Verification

Complete the application and follow the applicable document verification process.

  • Check your entered information.
  • Keep required documents available.
  • Complete the applicable verification procedure.
  • Make sure your application is properly confirmed.
Remember: Category or special candidature claims may require additional supporting documents.
03

Provisional & Final Merit List

The merit list helps determine your position for the admission process.

  • Check the provisional merit list.
  • Verify your name and details.
  • Raise a grievance if required within the prescribed period.
  • Check the final merit list.
Don't skip this: Always check the published merit information instead of assuming everything is correct.
04

Option Form & Preference Filling

Select your preferred colleges and courses and arrange them according to your preference.

  • Research colleges and branches before filling preferences.
  • Place your preferred options in the desired order.
  • Include suitable alternatives.
  • Review the complete preference list.
  • Confirm the option form within the prescribed period.
Important: Do not fill preferences randomly. Understand the college, branch and previous cutoff trends first.
05

CAP Seat Allotment

Seats are allotted through the Centralized Admission Process according to the applicable rules and your submitted preferences.

  • Check the allotment result.
  • Note the allotted institute and course.
  • Read the instructions applicable to your allotment.
  • Complete the required next step within the given schedule.
Current cycle: The 2026–27 process provides CAP Round I, II, III and IV allotment.
06

Seat Acceptance & College Admission

After allotment, complete the applicable seat acceptance and institute admission procedure.

  • Follow the seat acceptance instructions.
  • Report to the allotted institute as required.
  • Complete document verification.
  • Pay applicable fees.
  • Collect and safely store admission receipts/confirmation.
Final step: Your admission is completed only after completing the applicable institute-level requirements.

CENTRALIZED ADMISSION PROCESS

Understanding CAP Rounds

CAP allotment is conducted through computerized allotment. The current 2026–27 process includes four CAP rounds.

01

CAP Round I

Check your allotment and carefully read the instructions associated with your result.

Check → Accept / Proceed as applicable
02

CAP Round II

Candidates should check the new allotment and follow the applicable acceptance and reporting procedure.

Check → Follow current instructions
03

CAP Round III

Review the allotment carefully and complete the required next steps within the announced schedule.

Check → Complete required action
04

CAP Round IV

Check the final CAP round information and follow the official instructions for seat acceptance and admission.

Check → Complete admission
Important: CAP rules, schedules and candidate actions can depend on the current admission brochure and the type of allotment received. Always follow the latest official instructions.
DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents You May Need

The exact documents depend on your candidature, category and claims. Use this as a preparation checklist and verify the current official requirements.

Common Documents

Documents generally relevant to the admission process

SSC / 10th Marksheet

Keep your original SSC marksheet and required copies/details ready.

School Leaving Certificate

Keep the applicable School Leaving Certificate issued after SSC.

Nationality Proof

Keep the applicable document used to establish Indian nationality as required.

Other Qualifying Documents

HSC/Vocational or other documents may be required where applicable.

Category & Reservation Documents

Additional documents depend on the claim you are making

SC / ST

Caste / Tribe Certificate

Keep the applicable caste or tribe certificate if claiming the respective reservation benefit.

VJ / DT / NT

Caste + NCL

Applicable caste/tribe certificate and valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate where required.

OBC / SBC / SEBC

Caste + NCL

Applicable caste certificate and valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate where required.

EWS

EWS Certificate

Keep a valid EWS Eligibility Certificate when claiming the EWS benefit.

PwD

Disability Certificate

Applicable disability certificate/proforma required for the relevant claim.

ORPHAN

Orphan Certificate

Applicable certificate if claiming the orphan reservation benefit.

MINORITY

Minority Documents

Applicable minority declaration or supporting documents as required.

SPECIAL

Special Candidature Documents

Additional evidence may be required for specific candidature or special claims.

Candidature / Domicile Documents

Requirements depend on the candidature type claimed by the candidate.

Maharashtra Domicile

Applicable domicile evidence may be required depending on candidature type.

Parent / Candidate Documents

Certain candidature types may require prescribed documents relating to the candidate or parent.

Employment-Related Evidence

Applicable government employment or service documents may be required for certain candidature types.

Special Candidature Evidence

Certain special candidature types can require additional prescribed certificates or evidence.

Important Document Notice

Not every document listed above is required for every student. Requirements depend on your candidature, category, reservation claim and the current admission rules. Always verify the latest official DTE brochure before submitting your application.
