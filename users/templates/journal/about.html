ABOUT EDUGUIDE

Making College Decisions Simple & Smarter.

EduGuide is an admission guidance platform designed to help diploma students explore colleges, courses, previous cutoffs and admission possibilities in one place.

OUR PLATFORM

What is EduGuide?

Choosing the right college after completing a diploma can be confusing. Students often have to search through different sources for college information, courses and previous admission cutoffs.

EduGuide brings these resources together in one simple and student-friendly platform, helping students make more informed decisions about their higher education.

🎓

Your Admission Journey, Simplified.

Explore colleges, understand courses, check previous cutoffs and discover suitable admission options.

Helping Students Make Better Choices
