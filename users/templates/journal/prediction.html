What is College Prediction?

EduGuide's College Prediction system helps students understand which colleges they may have a chance of getting admission into based on their academic profile and preferences.

The system compares your profile with previous admission cutoff trends to provide useful college recommendations. It is designed to make the college selection process easier, clearer and more informed.

🎯

What Factors Are Considered?

01

Percentage

Your academic performance is an important factor in estimating admission chances.

02

Category

Category-wise cutoff trends are considered while matching your profile.

03

Preferred Branch

Your selected engineering branch is used to find relevant colleges.

04

Location & College Type

Your preferred location and college type help refine the recommendations.

05

Previous Cutoff Trends

Historical cutoff data helps estimate how closely your profile matches past admissions.
